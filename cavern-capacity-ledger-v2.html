03 · The enemy

A $3.5M to $7M decision, made without a reconciled number

Five things are moving the void-space numbers today.

1

Sonar interpretation is subjective

Third-party reads of the same trace can land 2.5% apart, both of them sound.

2

The remainder trap

Meter drift and unnoticed leak-off get categorised as solid waste.

3

Transfers between Caverns 1 and 2

Cavern 2 returns feed Cavern 1, so one undated movement shifts both balances.

4

A static 7:1 wash ratio

It describes a physical floor, and several planning numbers inherit it.

5

Unmodelled physical sinks

Insolubles refill washed space and salt creeps inward. Both shrink usable volume.

What it costs
19×

Gap between what a survey pair read and true void growth in our simulated cavern over eight months. Both readings were defensible. The return itself is fuzzy.

No cost per cubic metre to create space. None to deplete it. Nothing underneath the capital case.

The 19× figure comes from a two-year simulation using parameters inside the ranges discussed on 21 August 2026. Site operating data is not used.

08 · Method, in outline

What sits underneath this, and where it stops

What it is
  • A physical model of the cavern, with stratification, settling, dissolution and closure each carried on their own terms
  • One constraint that always has to close: oil plus brine plus settled solids plus reserve equals the mined void, at every point in time
  • Look-ahead filtering of the Kalman family, which carries uncertainty forward rather than discarding it
  • Validated the way a trading model is validated: walk-forward, out-of-sample, with confidence intervals reported
Where the work stops
  • Cavern engineering stays with the site and its contractors. That expertise is theirs
  • The call on any individual load stays with the operator
  • Existing systems stay where they are, and reconciliation runs alongside them
  • Misses get reported. Every figure arrives with the range around it
  • If the record will not carry the build, the study says so and recommends against it

The detail is the deliverable.
