Privacy & safety
Audio is limited to 90 seconds and 16 MB, downloaded transiently, converted locally, sent to OpenAI for transcription, and deleted after processing. Only transcript text can enter short session history. Confirmation expires after five minutes.

Act One

Field 121 investigation

Use real sanitized evidence to move from incident to field-team handoff.

6–7 min
“First, let’s investigate a real issue captured in the anonymized scouting snapshot.”
1

Establish the incident

What happened in Field 121?
Expected
An approved June 18 crop-health report, recorded hail damage, snapshot limitations, and a practical next check.
Presenter hint
Emphasize that the old report is evidence—not a claim about the live field condition.
Client value
A manager reaches the relevant source record without searching multiple dashboards.
2

Retrieve supporting evidence

Show scouting photos for Field 121.
Expected
Two sanitized scouting photos are attached once, without running visual analysis.
Presenter hint
Names, source URLs, coordinates, IDs, and embedded metadata have been removed.
Client value
Evidence travels with the conversation, reducing context switching.
3

Ask for AI visual assessment

Analyze the scouting photos for Field 121.
Expected
The backend provides the images internally to AI vision. WhatsApp receives an assessment without reattaching them.
Presenter hint
Say “visual decision support,” not diagnosis. Interpretation remains subject to human review.
Client value
Scouting evidence and source context are assessed together.

“This field” resolves to Field 121. The assistant recommends representative damaged and unaffected checks.
Presenter hint
Point out that the field alias is not repeated. This shows short-term conversational context.
Client value
The conversation remains natural while producing practical next steps.

Produce an operational handoff

A concise, uncertainty-aware message suitable for a field team.
Presenter hint
This closes the reasoning phase: issue → evidence → assessment → recommendation.

Management guidance becomes communication-ready in the same channel.

Present accurately

Guardrails & recovery

The strongest demo is ambitious about the experience and precise about what is simulated, historical, and private.

Say this

  • “Agricultural decision-support assistant.”
  • “One-time anonymized snapshot.”
  • “Visual assessment—not diagnosis.”
  • “Compressed simulated telemetry event.”
  • “Session-level operational memory.”
  • “Transient voice processing; transcript retained only in session.”

Presenter recovery notes

  • Wait for each AI response before continuing.
  • After a backend restart, begin again from Act One.
  • If context is lost, repeat the field alias explicitly.
  • If the alert does not appear, restart tracking and wait five seconds.
  • Never claim live weather, live machinery, or permanent storage.
