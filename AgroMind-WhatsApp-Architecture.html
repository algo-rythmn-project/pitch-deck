What each role can ask

Grower / agronomist

"Is it suitable to spray Field 081 now?"

AgroMind checks wind, temperature, humidity, forecast, product constraints, and field readiness, then explains whether to proceed, wait, or ask for agronomic approval.

The call stays with the grower, backed by evidence.
Controller / engineer

"Why are machine stops increasing today?"

AgroMind compares alert history, stop reasons, machines, fields, and maintenance context to find the pattern and recommend the next inspection.

Faster root cause, less downtime.
Manager

"What needs my attention across the operation?"

AgroMind prioritizes unresolved exceptions by affected hectares, duration, operational impact, ownership, and escalation status.

Attention goes where it matters.
Field team

"What should I inspect in this field?"

AgroMind combines scouting observations, imagery, field history, and current conditions into a short checklist and a follow-up action.

Clear next actions in the field.

How it fits. AgroMind sits alongside your dashboard and your WEED-IT hardware, it does not replace them. Your sensors, maps, and grower relationships stay yours. We add the communication channel and the reasoning that turns field signals into coordinated action.

Accuracy note: the demo proves the interaction pattern on real snapshot data with simulated operational events. Live telemetry, weather, and dashboard systems are integration targets until connectors, permissions, and operational policies are in place.
