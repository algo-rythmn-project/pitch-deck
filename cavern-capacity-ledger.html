02 · The problem

The residual: one figure carries every difference

Each of these is common across solution-mined disposal. Together they leave no cost per cubic metre behind the space that gets sold.

Sonar volume is interpreted, not measured

Two readings of the same trace can sit 2.5% apart, both of them sound. The instrument returns a signal, not a boundary — the wall position has to be read off a zone where the return is genuinely fuzzy.

Five mechanisms pull the three records apart

1

Sonar volume comes from interpreting a return

Two readings of one trace can sit 2.5% apart, both of them sound.

2

Solids are solved by difference

Meter drift, leak-off and undocumented transfers all resolve into one figure.

3

Two caverns share one balance by design

Cavern 2 returns feed Cavern 1, so a single transfer moves both books.

4

The 7:1 wash ratio carries planning weight

It describes the thermodynamic floor, and three planning numbers inherit it.

5

Two sinks rarely modelled anywhere

Insolubles refill new space and halite creeps closed. Both consume capacity.
