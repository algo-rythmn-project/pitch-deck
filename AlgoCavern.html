AlgoCompliance

Solution 2 · AlgoPricing

Prices each load on its net value.

  • Prices each load against two limited resources: how much injection capacity it uses and how much cavern space it permanently retires
  • Calculates the cost floor for each load, then sets the gate price above it — margin is explicit on every quote
  • Benchmarks against the customer's nearest alternative to price at the ceiling, not at cost
  • Load rankings shift as the facility fills — a blended rate cannot price both regimes correctly
Load value vs. gate price
Every load you take is worth something different to you. One flat rate can't do that.
Our tool prices each load where it lands, and it moves as oil and disposal prices move. Steady margin across the whole book.
These numbers just show the logic. On your real loads, that's what the pilot proves.
Decision support, not an automatic price. Inputs are the load composition and current market prices. The team sets the margin and signs off on every quote.
