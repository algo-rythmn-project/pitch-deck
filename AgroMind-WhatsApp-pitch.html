Not a generic chatbot: AgroMind can use the language model for conversation and reasoning, while agriculture knowledge and MCP/API tools ground the answer in live weather, customer policies, field information and machine context. Recommendations should show the evidence used, state uncertainty and keep the final operational decision with the responsible human.
Grower / agronomist“Is it suitable to spray Field 081 now?”
AgroMind checks: wind, temperature, humidity, forecast, product constraints and field readiness—then explains whether to proceed, wait or request agronomic approval.
Controller / engineer“Why are machine stops increasing today?”
AgroMind compares: alert history, stop reasons, machines, fields and maintenance context to identify patterns and recommend the next inspection.
Manager“What requires my attention across the operation?”
AgroMind prioritizes: unresolved exceptions, affected hectares, duration, operational impact, ownership and escalation status.
Field team“What should I inspect in this field?”
AgroMind combines: scouting observations, imagery, field history and current conditions into a concise checklist and follow-up action.
Concise business message

Two products that reinforce each other.

The WhatsApp proposition creates immediate operational reach. The AgroMind proposition adds longer-term differentiation through agriculture-specific conversation, analytics, recommendations and tool use.

Immediate proposition

Communication channel

Extend alerts from the customer's existing operations dashboard into WhatsApp for selected controllers, managers and other authorized roles.

  • No replacement of existing sensors or dashboard
  • Start with a few high-value alert types
  • Add interactive contact and acknowledgement
Strategic proposition

Agriculture intelligence

Give growers and specialists an agriculture-aware AI assistant that can answer questions using authorized customer data and live tools.

  • Natural-language access to complex information
  • Contextual recommendations with evidence
  • Role-specific analytics and operational actions
60-second presenter script
“Your existing platform already connects machines and field sensors to a strong operational dashboard. We are not proposing to replace that. Our first proposition is to add a WhatsApp channel to the existing alert flow, so controllers, managers and other authorized people can receive the right exceptions and take action wherever they are.

Our second proposition is AgroMind: a ChatGPT- or Claude-like AI agent designed for agriculture. It combines conversation with agricultural knowledge, customer-authorized data and live tools such as weather and maps. A grower can ask whether conditions are suitable for spraying; an engineer can investigate recurring stops; and a manager can ask what requires attention across the operation.

Together, the two propositions move the business from machine signals, to human understanding, to faster coordinated action.”
Accuracy note: the current demo proves the interaction pattern with simulated operational events and snapshot data. A production pitch should describe customer telemetry, weather and operational systems as integration targets until those live connectors, permissions and operational policies are implemented.
