The opportunity

In grain, the field shows its hand

Today's market runs on numbers that arrive late and models that watch only price. Meanwhile a farm's income is hostage to two forces — price and yield — and almost everyone insures against just one. That blind spot is worth billions.

Late information

Price-blind models

Half-covered farms

The space between what the field already knows and what the market has priced — that is where we win.
